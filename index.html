About me

I study Knowledge Engineering with a focus on AI, Data Mining, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing. I'm drawn to the messy middle — where formal knowledge structures meet noisy real-world data, and where getting the model right actually matters for people. Right now I'm especially interested in low-resource NLP for Burmese, a language that's underserved in the research literature.
